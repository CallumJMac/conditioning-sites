# Listening study: ethics self-assessment

Researcher: Callum Macpherson (independent researcher, callumjmac@outlook.com).
Assessed: November 2026. No institutional review board is available to an
independent researcher, so the study was assessed by the researcher against the
criteria commonly used to classify research as minimal risk.

## Study

Adult volunteers recruited on Prolific listen to pairs of short synthetic speech
clips (about 25 minutes) and rate which sounds more natural, or more like a
reference speaker. Details: `DEPLOY.md`.

## Assessment

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Participants | Adults (18+) recruited through Prolific; no vulnerable groups targeted |
| Task | Rating audio clips; no deception, no distressing content, no physical risk |
| Data collected | Prolific ID, screening answers (first language, hearing difficulties, playback device), ratings, timings, optional free-text comment, browser user agent |
| Personal data | No names, contact details, audio or video of participants; Prolific IDs are removed before any data are shared |
| Consent | Informed consent page before the study; participants can withdraw at any time by closing the page |
| Payment | GBP 4.50 for about 25 minutes (about GBP 11/h), above Prolific's recommended minimum |
| Voices | All rated voices imitate speakers from public, consented corpora (LibriTTS-R from LibriVox readers; Common Voice contributors); no real person is newly recorded or impersonated |
| Storage | Responses are stored in a Google Sheet owned by the researcher; published data are anonymised |

## Conclusion

The study meets the usual criteria for minimal-risk research: anonymous adult
participants, a non-sensitive rating task, no personal data beyond a platform ID,
informed consent, the right to withdraw and fair payment. The paper's ethics
statement reports that no institutional review was available and that this
self-assessment was carried out.
